# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project welcoming and respectful for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, considerate, and open to feedback.
- Focus discussion on the design and evidence behind a proposal.
- Respect differing experience levels and viewpoints.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, or publication of private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled fairly. Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict participation when this code is violated.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
